# Calder County Household Support Program

## Amendment No. 2026-01

**Issued:** 12 February 2026
**Effective:** 1 March 2026

*This amendment is issued under §1.2.3 of the Policy Manual. It amends the consolidated text as at 31 December 2025.*

---

## 1. Earnings disregard

**1.1** In §6.4.1(a), for "$120 per month" substitute "**$175 per month**".

## 2. Reporting of changes of circumstances

**2.1** In §4.3.2, for "10 calendar days" (in both places where it occurs) substitute "**14 calendar days**".

**2.2** In §9.1.4, for "30 calendar days" substitute "**14 calendar days**".

*Note for staff: the figure stated in §9.1.4 did not previously correspond to the requirement in §4.3.2. This amendment aligns the two. Determinations made before 1 March 2026 remain subject to §1.2.3 and to paragraph 5 below.*

## 3. Income thresholds

**3.1** In the table at §6.6.1, substitute the following —

| Household size | Monthly threshold |
|:--|:--|
| 1 | $1,225 |
| 2 | $1,650 |
| 3 | $2,075 |
| 4 | $2,500 |
| 5 | $2,925 |
| each additional member | + $425 |

## 4. Sanctions

**4.1** In §10.5.2, for "20 per cent" substitute "**15 per cent**".

**4.2** After §10.5.3, insert —

> **10.5.3A** A sanction must not be imposed in respect of a failure to report where the change of circumstances in question would have increased the award.

## 5. Transitional provision

**5.1** The amendments made by paragraphs 1, 3 and 4 apply to any determination made on or after 1 March 2026, including a determination in respect of a period before that date.

**5.2** The amendments made by paragraph 2 apply **only in respect of a change of circumstances occurring on or after 1 March 2026**. Where the change of circumstances occurred before 1 March 2026, the reporting period is the period that applied at the date of the change, irrespective of the date of the determination.

**5.3** Where a claim relates to a period spanning 1 March 2026, the applicable figures are those in force on each day of the period, and the award is apportioned accordingly under §7.4.3.

---

*End of Amendment No. 2026-01.*
